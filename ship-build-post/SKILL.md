# ship-build-post

## When
After 2–3 related merged PRs, or one slice that is worth a single post. Not after every PR.

## Do
1. Read the PR titles, bodies, and the user-facing change. English sources only.
2. Write **one** X draft. Invite a reply. Not a changelog, not a feature list.
3. Match Oscar’s voice if recent X posts were sampled; otherwise plain English. Vietnamese is for chat, not the post, unless he asked.
4. Attach 1–2 **real** screenshots of what shipped (running app, schema, compose, UI). Do not generate mock UI.
5. Show Oscar the draft and images. He edits and posts.

## Do not
- Post or schedule to X
- Auto-like, auto-reply, or farm engagement
- Invent screens or GenerateImage stand-ins
- Leak secrets, seed passwords, private URLs, or customer data
- Use retired names (disti, disti-user, bare “user” / “company”) when the post is about CloudBSS parties

## Done
Oscar has a draft + images in chat. Stop.
